Excel Challenge – User Data Deletion

Effective date: October 4, 2026

How to request deletion

Step 1: Send an email to ramesh.aithaa@gmail.com with the subject line Excel Challenge – Data Deletion Request.
Step 2: Include the name or platform account information associated with your use of Excel Challenge, so we can identify the relevant account.
Step 3: We may request reasonable information needed to verify that the request is authorized.

What happens next?

We will review the request and delete eligible personal information associated with the app, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or platform-compliance reasons.

Important

Deleting app data may remove game progress, scores, achievements, or other information associated with your account. Some information controlled directly by Facebook or another platform may need to be deleted through that platform's own account or privacy controls.

Contact

For questions about deletion, contact ramesh.aithaa@gmail.com.
